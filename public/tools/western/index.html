Make the running order your own.

Bring the ceremony, speeches and music into one clear working plan. These tools organise your choices; your celebrant, venue and suppliers should confirm the final timing and arrangements.

Your entries stay on this page. Nothing is submitted, stored or shared. Keep a copy before leaving or reloading, and leave private contact details and access codes out of the brief.

01 / The ceremony

A clear order, with room to breathe.

Set a start time, then add the moments you have agreed with the people involved. The example durations are editable planning placeholders. Confirm entrances, legal or ceremonial requirements, access and any transition time with your actual team.

Write each line as Moment | minutes. Use 1–240 minutes per moment. Change the names, sequence and durations to match your celebration.

02 / The speeches

The right introduction. The agreed handover.

List only the people who have agreed to speak. Confirm names, pronunciation, microphones and any language support with your host. Leave space for introductions and transitions in the durations you enter; no particular family or cultural order is assumed.

Write each line as Name or handover | minutes. Agree a comfortable length with each speaker and check the whole block against meal service.

03 / The music

A brief your musicians can use.

Choosing a DJ, live musicians or both is a conversation about your venue, event moments and the work you need. Record the exact track or arrangement, version and cue where known. Ask the provider to confirm availability, sound needs and the agreed scope.
