Wedding planners: why should couples shortlist your team?

Before we consider a profile, we want to understand what your team does well. Send us a private introduction in your own words.

Give us one example

Tell us about one decision that made a wedding day run better. A venue changed the load-in time, a ceremony moved indoors, or two family traditions needed room in the same timeline. What did your team change, and what did the couple experience as a result?

Tell us where your firm fits

Include your planning company, website, home market, travel area and the services you personally deliver. Distinguish full planning from design or day-of coordination. If you mention a certification, name the holder and issuer. Link to a portfolio you have permission to share.

Make your case

A short email with your example and one supporting link is a good start. We’ll use it to consider your firm for a future profile. Sending it does not open an account, publish a listing, reserve a place or trigger a payment.
