Top 100 wedding songs for 2026

For the first dance, our first pick is Etta James’s “At Last.” For the moment everyone else joins you, it’s Earth, Wind & Fire’s “September.” The best song changes with the job you give it.

This is the Weddings.io editorial playlist for couples planning a 2026 wedding. We chose recordings for ten moments and moods, drawing from several decades. The numbering helps you brief your DJ; it does not measure streams or bookings.
